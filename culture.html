OUR HERITAGE

A Culture Full of Life

Gujarat is known for its welcoming people, beautiful traditions, colourful clothes, folk music and lively celebrations.

Navratri Festival

Navratri is one of Gujarat's most famous festivals. People wear traditional clothes and perform Garba and Dandiya Raas to celebrate.

Garba & Folk Dance

Garba is a traditional Gujarati dance performed in circles, especially during Navratri, with rhythmic music and energetic movements.

Art & Handicrafts

Gujarat is famous for embroidered textiles, Bandhani, Patola silk, mirror work and beautiful handicrafts made by skilled local artisans.

A TASTE OF GUJARAT

Famous Gujarati Foods

From tasty snacks to traditional meals, Gujarati cuisine offers a wonderful mix of flavours.

Dhokla

A soft, steamed snack made mainly from fermented batter.

Thepla

A popular Gujarati flatbread, often flavoured with spices and fenugreek.

Khandvi

Delicate rolls made from gram flour and yoghurt, seasoned with spices.

Undhiyu

A traditional mixed-vegetable dish especially enjoyed in winter.

Fafda & Jalebi

A much-loved combination of savoury crispy fafda and sweet jalebi.

Gujarati Thali

A complete meal featuring dishes such as roti, dal, vegetables, rice and sweets.

Atithi Devo Bhava

“A guest is like God” — a value that reflects India's tradition of hospitality.
